    JEE Main exam pattern & marking scheme

    What your JEE Main mocks need to mirror

    JEE Main Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) is a 3-hour computer-based test with 75 questions worth 300 marks, 25 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Every question counts: correct answers earn +4, wrong answers cost −1. Mock tests only build exam temperament if they copy this pattern exactly.

    for each correct answer, in both MCQs and numerical value questions

    0

    for questions left unanswered or marked for review without an answer

    Key facts at a glance

    Conducted by
    National Testing Agency (NTA)
    Mode
    Computer-based test (CBT) at exam centres
    Duration
    3 hours (4 hours for eligible PwD candidates)
    Sessions
    Two sessions a year, typically January and April; the better NTA score counts
    Numerical questions
    All 5 per subject are compulsory; the answer is entered on an on-screen keypad
    Leads to
    NIT, IIIT and GFTI admissions, and eligibility for JEE Advanced (IIT admissions)

    Based on the NTA information bulletin for recent sessions. Always confirm the current year's pattern on the official NTA JEE Main website.

    How to use mock tests well in the final months

    • Simulate the real slot. Same 3-hour window, same timer, no breaks, on a computer rather than paper.
    • Analyse longer than you test. The marks are made in the review: sort every wrong answer into concept, calculation, or time.
    • Practise skipping. With −1 per wrong answer, knowing which questions to leave is a skill that only mocks can train.
    • Track chapters, not just totals. A flat total can hide a rising Maths score and a falling Chemistry one.

    FAQ

    Questions coaching institutes ask us

    How do I conduct online JEE Main mock tests for my coaching institute?

    Sign up for EdisonOS, add your batches and students, then build a JEE Main mock test from your own questions (or import your PDF papers with the AI Question Extractor) and assign it. Students take it in the browser, and faculty get a score report with subject, chapter and difficulty analysis for each student as soon as they submit.

    Can I run the JEE test series under my institute's own brand?

    Yes. EdisonOS is white-label: you can add your logo and favicon, run the portal on your own domain or an edisonos.com subdomain, and send emails from your own authenticated sender address. Students and parents see your institute, not ours.

    Can I convert my existing PDF question papers and DPPs into online tests?

    Yes. The AI Question Extractor (currently in beta) reads a PDF and turns it into structured questions you can review, tag by chapter and save to your question bank, then use in mock tests or DPPs.

    Is there an NTA-style test interface for JEE Main on EdisonOS?

    An NTA-style test screen for JEE Main is coming soon to EdisonOS, mirroring the real exam with a question palette, mark for review, numerical answer entry and +4 / −1 marking. Talk to sales to see the roadmap and join early access.

    Do students need to install any software to take the test?

    No. Tests run in a web browser on a laptop or desktop. Before starting, a system-check screen confirms the browser, connection and display are ready, so students are not surprised mid-test.

    What analysis do faculty get after each JEE mock test?

    For every attempt you see the score, accuracy, time per question and gap to target; performance by subject, chapter and difficulty; performance by time spent; answer changes; a skill snapshot of strongest and weakest areas; and session logs. Batch reports show average, median, highest and lowest scores, and progress against each student's target.

    Can parents see their child's JEE preparation progress?

    Yes. Parents get a read-only dashboard showing their child's scores, coursework, chapter-wise performance and review history, and they can be notified when a test is started or completed and before scheduled tests.

    Can I create batches and study plans for JEE students?

    Yes. Create a group for each batch, add students and faculty, and attach a study plan of DPPs, resources and mock tests that unlock all at once, one after another or on a schedule, each with its own due date.

    What is the JEE Main exam pattern for Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech)?

    JEE Main Paper 1 is a 3-hour computer-based test with 75 questions worth 300 marks: 25 each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Each subject has 20 multiple-choice questions and 5 numerical value questions, all compulsory. A correct answer earns +4 and an incorrect answer −1.

    How is EdisonOS priced for coaching institutes?
